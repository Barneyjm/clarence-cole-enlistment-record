First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

One card on two sheets, not two cards. The left sheet foots PAGE 1 OF 2 and the
right is headed CONTINUATION and foots PAGE 2 OF 2. The change table runs across
both: the first group line breaks mid-sentence at the foot of sheet 1 and its
last words open the continuation.

Eight enlisted men come in from the 551st FA Bn. Their group line, verbatim:
`Above 8 EM asgd & jd fr 551st FA Bn APO 758 US Army per par 7 SO 83 Hq 551st FA
Bn dtd 27 Aug 45 (EDCMR 28 Aug 45)`. Rows carry the distilled action, with each
man's Race, MCO, ASR and Phy Cl from the second line of his entry.

The two men returning from temporary duty carry the group line `Above 2 EM fr TD
to dy`.

Weitz is typed 32826608, the serial carried on frames 163, 165, 170, 177, 202
and 203. He was transferred out of the battery to the 965th FA Bn on 18 June 45
and returns here from the 551st.

Lambert is typed James L, as on frame 243. Frame 208 types James I. His serial
34173673 and Wilczynski's 33474131 match frames 243, 198 and 200. Both went to
Lyons on 19 August, frame 243.

Asay's surname is over-inked at the third character. Stelts is typed with a
lower-case middle initial, `Oscar h`.

The last digit of Thorp's MOS runs into the edge of the card. It reads 1193,
which is the MOS carried on frame 211 by 1st Lt Bassett, the officer transferred
to the 551st in July. Thorp's ASR figure is followed by two characters struck
out with hyphens.

Stelts, Asay, Moore, Sheehan, Arnold, Pearce, Southall and Thorp are new to the
record.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events, and it balances: 2 candidates and 37 essentials against 39 enlisted
men assigned. Frame 253, the day before, gives 31 enlisted men assigned.
