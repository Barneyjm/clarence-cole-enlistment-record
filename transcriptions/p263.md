First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards. The 12 September card is handwritten and is a single sheet footed
PAGE 1 OF 1. The 13 September card is typed and is footed PAGE 1 OF 2; its
continuation sheet is on frame 264, and that sheet carries the record of events
and the redeployment tally for the day. Both are signed GORDON F KERKHOFF,
Capt, FA.

The 13 September card opens with a correction, verbatim:

> CORRECTION (1? Sept 45)
> Officers strength section  Capt Asgd No entry Total No entry For dy No entry
> 1st Lt Asgd no entry Total No entry TD-DS No entry  Total Asgd No entry Total
> No entry For dy No entry TD-DS No entry
> SHOULD BE
> Officers strength section  Capt Asgd 1 Total 1 For dy 1  1st Lt Asgd 1 Total 1
> TD-DS 1
> Total Asgd 2  Total 2  For dy 1  TD-DS 1

Only the top stroke of the second digit of that date printed, so it is left as a
question mark. The officers rows of the printed strength grid on the 12
September card on this frame are blank, which is the state the correction
describes.

A handwritten mark sits above the word `strength` in the SHOULD BE line and
cannot be read.

Watson's 33217931 matches frames 52, 133 and 257. Brown's 32631774 matches
frames 235, 257 and the 13 September card on this frame. Frame 257 sent both men
on a seven-day furlough to Switzerland as of 1 September; they return to duty
here. The 12 September group line, verbatim: `Above 2 EM Fr Fur To dy`. Rows
carry the distilled action.

Steltz is typed with a final z on the 13 September card. Frame 254, his
assignment card, types `Stelts, Oscar h`. Both readings are kept where they
fall. His serial 16024599 and MOS 521 match that frame.

Webber's 33559054 matches frame 234, which gives his MOS as 521. This card gives
786. Both readings are kept where they fall.

Pearce's 35609475 and Southall's 33519183 match frame 254, as do their MOS 845.

The 13 September group line runs off the foot of this sheet: `Above 5 EM trfd to
2nd Armd Div Arty APO Unknown US Army per par 2 SO 75 Hq 153rd FA`. Frame 264
carries the rest of it — `Bn APO 758 dtd 13 Sept 45 Departed (EDCMR 13 Sept
45)`. Rows carry the distilled action.

The code column on the 13 September card carries handwritten marks: a `K` and a
stroke against Steltz, a vertical stroke spanning Webber and Brown, and a
diagonal stroke against Southall.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed.
