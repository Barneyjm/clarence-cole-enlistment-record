First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

One card on two sheets, not two cards. The left sheet is footed PAGE 1 OF 2, the
right is headed CONTINUATION and footed PAGE 2 OF 2 and repeats the 22 September
header and the station line. The change table is all on sheet 1; sheet 2 carries
only the record of events.

The station is Camp Pittsburg Assembly Area APO 752, spelled without the h. It is
the first station of that name in the transcribed frames. The card carries no map
citation and no grid reference.

The change table opens with a correction naming Metz, verbatim: `CORRECTION (14
Sept to 21 Sept 45, incl) Metz Robert J 35713328 PFC 844 ERR picked up on par 1
SO 226 Hq 29th Inf Div APO 29 as PFC SHOULD BE Metz Robert J 35713328 Pvt 844`.
Frame 269, the SO 226 page that lists him, carries him as Pfc. His row here
carries the corrected grade.

Metz's serial is written twice on the card. The second writing is clear. The
second digit of the first is a blot; it is read against the second line and
against frame 269, which types 35713328.

Lambert's 34173673 matches frames 208, 243 and 254.

A second correction restates the 21 September redeployment tally, verbatim:

> CORRECTION (21 Sept 45)
> Candidates 4 -
> Essentials - - 106
> Tot Asgd 4 - 107
> SHOULD BE
> Candidates 4 - 102
> Essentials - - 5
> Tot Asgd 4 - 107

The enlisted figure on the Candidates line of the erroneous block is not entered;
the O / WO / EM column headings are written across that line.

Signed GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower bands
and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record of
events.
