First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each. Both are handwritten, both are single sheets footed
PAGE 1 OF 1, and both are signed GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA.

Neither card names a man, so neither carries rows. The 8 September card is a
correction to the officers strength section of the 7 September card; the
9 September card is written NO CHANGE.

The 8 September correction, verbatim:

> CORRECTION (7 Sept 45)
> OFFICERS STRENGTH SECTION.
> Capt TD & DS 1  1ST LT TD & DS NO ENTRY
> SHOULD BE
> OFFICERS STRENGTH SECTION
> Capt TD & DS NO ENTRY. 1ST LT TD & DS 1

The ampersand is written as a plus sign throughout that block.

The station line is written `wG 7875` on both cards, lower-case w against a
capital G.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events on both cards. The enlisted figures on the 8 September card are
written over earlier figures and the strikeouts underneath cannot be read; the
final readings match the 9 September card. Both cards give 2 officers and 35
enlisted men assigned. Frame 258, four days earlier, gives 4 officers and 38
enlisted men assigned.
