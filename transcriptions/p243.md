First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

One card on two sheets, not two cards. The right-hand sheet is headed
CONTINUATION and foots PAGE 2 OF 2 against PAGE 1 OF 2 on the left, and repeats
the 20 August header and the Herzhausen station line. The change table runs
across both.

Sgt Cole is the first row. This is the 20 August card, previously held only as a
separately supplied image; frames 1 through 242 do not name him.

Cole's entry types `Approx suration 5 days`. The group entry four lines below it
types `duration`. Both are kept as typed.

Chmielewski is typed 35761991 here. Frames 2, 4 and 113 carry 35261991 for the
same man. The third digit is the contested one; this exposure is clear and both
readings are kept where they fall.

A pen stroke crosses the first letter of Tierce's surname and the second and
third digits of his serial. Frames 233, 237, 239, 246 and 250 carry Tierce,
Jasper B at 34332726, which settles both.

Lambert is typed James L here and James I. on frame 208.

The Lyons group line, verbatim: `Above 3 EM fr dy to TD Lyons, France per Ltr
Hq 32nd FA Brig (Lv Fur & Passes) dtd 16 Aug 45 Departed as of 19 Aug 45 Approx
duration 5 days`. Rows carry the distilled action.

Lambert appears twice: once in the Lyons group, once at the foot of sheet 1
against the ASR change that opens the continuation sheet. Holland's 95 to 100
and Zouski's 84 to 89 are confirmed by their ASR figures on frames 248 and 252.
A diagonal pen stroke runs from the end of each ASR line into the empty space to
its right.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events.
