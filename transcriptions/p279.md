First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each, both single sheets footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and both signed
GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA. Both change tables are written NO CHANGE, so
neither card carries rows.

The station is spelled `Camp Pittsburgh` on both cards, as on the 28 September
card of frame 278. The station line on the 30 September card is the fainter of
the two; the place name is legible, and the APO number at its right-hand end is
read against the 29 September card beside it. Neither card carries a map citation
or a grid reference.

The redeployment tally is the same on both cards and carries forward the 109
enlisted men of 28 September on frame 278. The Essentials line is a dash on both.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events on both cards.
