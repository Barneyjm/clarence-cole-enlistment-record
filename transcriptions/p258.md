First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each. Both are single sheets footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and both are
signed GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA.

Moore's 35724457 and MOS 845 match frame 254, which brought him in from the
551st FA Bn on 28 August. He leaves the battery six days later.

Lovelace is typed 35798290 here. Frame 207, his assignment card, carries
35294290 for the same man, `Lovelace, Billy K`. The third and fifth digits are
the contested ones; this exposure reads 7 and 8. Both readings are kept where
they fall.

Simonin's 37723822 matches frames 133, 155, 168 and 247. Frame 247 sent him on a
seven-day furlough to Switzerland as of 25 August; he returns to duty here.

Krol, Brimlow and Cook are new to the record.

Brimlow goes to Battery A and Cook comes from it on the same card, both by verbal
order of the battalion. Cook is carried as not yet joined because he was on
temporary duty with the 79th FA Gp.

Cook's ASR entry types `ASR 73-No-` and stops there, with a pen stroke under it.
Brimlow's MOS is typed 0200 and Cook's 5000.

The station line on the 4 September card opens with three or four characters
struck out by overtyping, then the Geismar line. The characters underneath cannot
be read.

The code column carries handwritten marks against four of the rows: `K` and a
stroke against Moore, `12` and a stroke against Krol, a stroke against Lovelace,
`6` against Brimlow, and `B` and a stroke against Cook.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events on both cards, and it balances: 3 candidates and 1 essential against 4
officers assigned, 2 candidates and 36 essentials against 38 enlisted men
assigned. Frame 257 gives 39 enlisted men assigned; Moore's transfer is the
difference.
