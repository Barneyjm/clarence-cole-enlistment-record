First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

One card on two sheets, not two cards. The left sheet foots PAGE 1 OF 2 and the
right is headed CONTINUATION and foots PAGE 2 OF 2. The change table is all on
sheet 1; the continuation carries the record of events and the redeployment
tally and nothing else.

Sgt Cole is on this card. He is transferred out of Battery C to the 3rd Reinf
Depot with Chavez and Wilczynski, EDCMR 5 September 1945. His serial 36106875,
grade Sgt and MOS 845 are all clean, and the serial matches frames 243 and 246.
This is the third card on the film that names him.

Mulcahy leaves on the same order, by a different paragraph of it. His serial
O1178574 matches frame 218, his assignment card. The MOS column reads 1193 here;
frame 219, the cleaner exposure of his assignment, reads 1183 in that column
against `Duty 1193` in the body of the entry.

Chavez's 18016795 matches frames 173, 193, 230, 233, 242 and 247. Wilczynski's
33474131 matches frames 198, 200, 243 and 254.

The two pigeon men go back to their own company. Frame 198 attached them to the
battery from the 285th Signal Pigeon Co on 10 June 45. That frame types the
surnames `Comune` and `DeLuca`; this frame types `Conune`, with two legs on the
third character, and `DeLuca`. The serials agree with frame 198 on both men:
32453936 and 32232305.

The officer group line, verbatim: `Reld asgmt & trfd to 3rd Reinf Depot APO 153
US Army per par 2 SO 71 Hq 153rd FA Bn APO 758 dtd 5 Sept 45 Departed (EDCMR 5
Sept 45)`. The enlisted group line is the same order at paragraph 1: `Above 3 EM
trfd to 3rd Reinf Depot APO 153 US Army per par 1 SO 71 Hq 153rd FA Bn APO 758
dtd 5 Sept 45 Departed (EDCMR 5 Sept 45)`. The pigeon group line: `Above 2 EM
Reld atchd & returned 285th Signal Pigeon Co APO 655 US Army`. Rows carry the
distilled action.

The code column carries a handwritten mark like a K against Mulcahy and against
Cole, a stroke against Chavez and Wilczynski, and `-4` written below the last
row.

The word `Zone` in the station line on the continuation sheet is over-inked at
the first character. The station is otherwise the same on both sheets.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events, and it balances: 2 candidates and 1 essential against 3 officers
assigned, 1 candidate and 34 essentials against 35 enlisted men assigned. Frame
258 gives 4 officers and 38 enlisted men. The four transfers on this card are the
difference; the two pigeon men were attached rather than assigned and do not
appear in the figures.
