First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

One card on two sheets, not two cards. The right-hand sheet is headed
CONTINUATION and foots PAGE 2 OF 2 against PAGE 1 OF 2 on the left, and repeats
the 21 August header and the Herzhausen station line. It carries the map
citation and the redeployment tally; the change table and the record of events
are all on sheet 1.

The card is a day of returns. The four men off furlough are the four sent to
Nancy on 15 August (frame 240). Holland and Lolmaugh went to DS with the 3rd QM
Bn on 4 August (frame 232). Rappoccio's TD began on 18 August (frame 242).

Two characters of George's first name are lost to a break in the ink; `To` and
`as E` survive and admit only Tobias. The seventh digit of his serial is cut by
the printed rule and does not resolve on this exposure — 5 and 8 both fit what
survives. Frames 106, 107, 135, 153 and 240 read 33082681, as does his SO 226
row on frame 248.

The other nine serials match the record: Olson, Paige and Reynolds against
frame 240, Holland and Lolmaugh against frame 232, Plunkett against frames 226
and 250, Baroglio against frame 232, Rappoccio against frames 239 and 242, and
Turnbaugh against frames 147 and 227.

The three group lines, verbatim: `Above 4 EM fr fur (5 days) to dy`,
`Above 2 EM fr DS to dy`, `Above 4 EM fr TD to dy`. Rows carry the distilled
action.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events.
