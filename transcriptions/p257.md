First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each. Both are single sheets footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and both are
signed GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA. The 2 September card is typed NO CHANGE and
carries no entries; it has no RECORD OF EVENTS heading, and the map citation and
the redeployment tally are typed in the body of the change table.

1st Lt Thorp joins. Frame 254 carries him as `Asgd & not yet jd fr 578th FA Bn`
with EDCMR 27 Aug 45, ASR 89 and MOS 1193; this card records the joining and
repeats ASR 89. His serial O1182727 and MOS 1193 match that frame.

Thorp's duty entry types four characters after `Duty`. The first two are `Co` and
the last is `C`; the third did not print cleanly and is left as a question mark.
Frame 254 types his duty as `Unknown`.

The trailer to his ASR figure reads `89-Q-No-3?.8`. The digit before the decimal
point is over-inked and is left as a question mark.

The code column against Thorp's row carries a handwritten `3` and a stroke.

Watson's 33217931 matches frames 52 and 133. Brown's 32631774 matches frame 235,
his assignment card.

The furlough group line, verbatim: `Above 2 EM fr dy to fur (7 days)
Switzerland per Ltr Hq 32nd FA Brig Lv Fur & Passes dtd 16 Aug 45`. Rows carry
the distilled action. The day of the letter date prints faintly; frame 243
carries a Hq 32nd FA Brig leave letter dated 16 Aug 45.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events on both cards, and it balances: 3 candidates and 1 essential against 4
officers assigned, 2 candidates and 37 essentials against 39 enlisted men
assigned. Frame 254, four days earlier, gives 3 officers assigned. Thorp's
joining is the difference.
