First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

The left sheet is a typed CONTINUATION of the 6 July report begun on frame 214,
headed CONTINUATION and footing PAGE 2 OF 2 PAGES. The right sheet is the 7 July
card, handwritten and signed Gordon F. Kerkhoff, CAPT FA. Frame 214 records
Kerkhoff joining as battery commander on 6 July.

The 6 July group line, verbatim: `Above 3 EM asgd & jd fr 893rd TD Bn APO 758 US
Army per par 1 SO 85 Hq 893rd TD Bn APO 758 dtd 5 July 45 (EDCMR 6 July 45)`.
Rows carry the distilled action. Only two men are on this sheet; the third is
Wysochansky, on the first sheet at frame 214, where the source unit is not given.
Frame 214 records three men transferred to the 893rd TD Bn the day before.

Mays is smudged at the fifth digit of his serial. Frame 250 reads 6943520, with
MOS 345, MCO 245 and ASR 97 — the same three figures this card carries.

The first letter of Rappoccio's surname is over-inked and could be B or R. His
serial 32341806 is confirmed by frames 237, 239 and 242, and his MOS 345, MCO 302
and ASR 87 match frame 251, which reads the name Rappocdio.

Squires is transferred out to the 692nd FA Bn under par 1 SO 77; frame 216 has
McConnell coming in from the 692nd under the same paragraph of the same order.
His serial is written in a hand whose 8s, 4s and 9s are hard to separate, and
only `334?37` is legible. Frames 133 and 137 carry 33403788 for Squires, John J.

The last three digits of Van Bibber's serial are written over an erasure. Frame
208 reads 37388645.

The typed sheet's place name is over-inked at the fourth letter and is read here
as Hershausen, as on frame 226; the handwritten card writes Herzhausen.

Strength grids are not transcribed on this page — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed.
