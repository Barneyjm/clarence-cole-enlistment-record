First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two handwritten cards, one sheet each, both footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and signed GORDON
F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA. Both change tables read NO CHANGE and carry no names.

These are the first cards of the move west. The station line on both is `Enroute
To Assembly Area`, with no place name and no grid reference, and neither record
of events carries a map citation. The battery was at Geismar on 17 September
(frame 272).

The town is written Kaiserslautern on the 18 September card and Kaiserlautern on
the 19th.

The strength figures are unchanged across both cards and unchanged from 17
September: 4 officers and 107 enlisted men assigned.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower bands
and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record of
events on both cards.
