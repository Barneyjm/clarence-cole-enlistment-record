First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

One card on a single sheet, footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and signed GORDON F KERKHOFF,
Capt, FA. The frame also holds a filming spacer card printed SPACER, once upright
and once inverted, footed `AG RAC M P 1 OCT 47 4-38`, and a strip of the
30 August card down the left edge. That card is frame 255; no rows are repeated
from it.

Both men return from the Treebek leave that began on 25 August, frame 247. Their
group line, verbatim: `Above 2 EM fr TD to dy`. Rows carry the distilled action.

The surname is typed Braeger here, Breager on frame 247 and Breeger on frame 162.
The serial 36260397 is the same on all three. Johnstad's 36250465 matches frames
46, 133 and 247; the fifth digit prints faintly on this card.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events, and it balances: 2 candidates and 37 essentials against 39 enlisted
men assigned, 3 candidates and 1 essential against 4 officers assigned. The
figures are unchanged from frames 255 and 257.
