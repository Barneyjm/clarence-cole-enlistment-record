First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two sheets carrying two different reports. The left sheet is headed CONTINUATION
and foots PAGE 2 OF 2; it holds the rest of the 28 June change table, whose page
1 is on frame 208. The right sheet is the 29 June card, handwritten, PAGE 1 OF 1,
signed Charles R Wood Jr, 1st Lt FA.

The 28 June sheet is typed and the ink is uneven; several serials print faintly
in places. The 29 June card carries `NO CHANGE` across the change table and
records no men.

The 28 June group line, verbatim: `Above 12 EM trfd to 268th FA Bn APO 758 US
Army per par 1 SO 46 Hq 153rd FA Bn APO 758 dtd 28 June 45 Departed (EDCMR 28
June 45)`. Rows carry the distilled action. Five of the twelve men are on this
sheet; the other seven are on page 1, frame 208, where the card gives the group
action without naming the receiving unit. The 268th FA Bn is the unit six men
joined Battery C from on 27 June, also on frame 208.

Robinson's first character is blotted. The remaining seven digits give 1302531,
and frames 163, 165 and 169 read 31302531 for him.

Roman's last digit does not print. Frames 198 and 200 read 33403922 for him.

Saunders's fourth digit does not print. Frame 198 reads 15339562 for him.

Rauscher and Rossetti are new to the record. Rossetti's serial is faint through
the middle and is flagged.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures are left rather
than guessed.
