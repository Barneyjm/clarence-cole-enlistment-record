First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

One card on two sheets, not two cards. The right-hand sheet is headed
CONTINUATION and foots PAGE 2 OF 2 against PAGE 1 OF 2 on the left, and repeats
the 26 August header and the Geismar station line. The change table begins on
sheet 1 and its last entry runs over onto sheet 2.

This is the day Special Orders 66 took effect. 2d Lt Nesbitt and 29 enlisted men
left for the 70th Inf Div, EDCMR 26 August 1945. The card gives Nesbitt his own
row; the 29 enlisted men are recorded only as a count, with the order attached.
Frames 248 to 252 carry SO 66. The entry, verbatim across the two sheets:
`29 EM trfd to 70th Inf Div APO 70 US Army per par 1 SO 66 Hq 153rd FA Bn APO
758 dtd 24 Aug 45 Departed (EDCMR 26 Aug 45) Copy atchd`.

The tally records the result. On the 25 August card, frame 246, it stood at 3 O
and 67 EM assigned; here it is 2 O and 38 EM. Both figures balance against the
departures.

The battery also moved. The station changes from Herzhausen to Geismar, and the
record of events gives the distance. The battery held Geismar from May to July;
the reference wG7875 matches the G7875 typed through that stay, now with the
lower-case zone letter the clerk adopted at Herzhausen.

Johnstad and Breager go to Treebek on the same 32nd FA Brigade leave letter of
16 August that sent Sgt Cole there on the 20 August card, frame 243. Their group
line, verbatim: `Above 2 EM fr dy to TD Treebek Holland per Ltr Hq 32nd FA Brig
(Lv Fur & Passes) dtd 16 Aug 45 Approx duration 5 days Departed as of 25 Aug 45`.
Rows carry the distilled action.

Chavez returns from the TD at the Oberammergau school that began on 18 August,
frame 242.

Nesbitt's serial O2008362 matches frames 197 and 212, and his SO 66 row on frame
248, which types it O-2008362. Johnstad's 36250465 matches frames 46 and 133.
Breager's 36260397 matches frame 162, which spells the name Breeger. Simonin's
37723822 matches frames 133, 155 and 168. Chavez's 18016795 matches frames 173,
193, 230, 233 and 242.

Two characters did not print. The leading digit of `70th` on Nesbitt's line is
gone; the 29 EM entry on the same card types `70th Inf Div APO 70` plainly. The
final letter of `Switzerland` on Simonin's line printed as a trace.

The code column on Nesbitt's row carries a pen stroke rather than typed
characters, and a second pen stroke crosses the strength grid below it.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events.
