First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Strength grids not transcribed on this page — the figures sit in the lower bands
and are left rather than guessed.

Both cards are typed. The 24 July card confirms Goad against frame 224: same
serial 6251565, and it types his MOS as 824 where the handwritten card was
ambiguous. The 25 July card is a heavy over-inked exposure; frame 227 carries
the same two cards in a cleaner exposure, and these rows are corrected against
it. Plunkett's 38117068 matches frame 250. Turnbaugh's serial is carried two
ways elsewhere (33986924 on frame 46, 33386924 on frame 147); both exposures
here read 33386924, siding with frame 147. Baroglio is new to the record; this
exposure suggests 0 for his last serial digit, frame 227 reads 9, and the typed
4 August card on frame 232 settles it as 39577769.

The 25 July group line, verbatim: `Above 3 EM fr dy to TD with 3rd QM Bn per
VOCO 32nd FA Brig APO 758 US Army approx duration 7 days Departed`. Rows carry
the distilled action.
