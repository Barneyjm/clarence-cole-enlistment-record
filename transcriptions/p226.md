First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Strength grids not transcribed on this page — the figures sit in the lower bands
and are left rather than guessed.

Both cards are typed. The 24 July card confirms Goad against frame 224: same
serial 6251565, and it types his MOS as 824 where the handwritten card was
ambiguous. The 25 July card is a heavy over-inked exposure and its serial
numbers are part blob — each unreadable digit is a `?`, not a guess. For what
the cleaner sources say: Plunkett appears on frame 250 as 38117068, consistent
with every legible digit here; Turnbaugh's serial is already carried two ways
(33986924 on frame 46, 33386924 on frame 147), and the legible digits here fit
the frame-147 reading — in particular the third digit is no 9. Baraglio is new
to the record.

The 25 July group line, verbatim: `Above 3 EM fr dy to TD with 3rd QM Bn per
VOCO 32nd FA Brig APO 758 US Army approx duration 7 days Departed`. Rows carry
the distilled action.
