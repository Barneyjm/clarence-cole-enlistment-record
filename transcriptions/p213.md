First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

The left sheet is a typed CONTINUATION of the 3 July report begun on frame 212.
It is headed CONTINUATION and foots PAGE 2 OF 2 PAGES. The right sheet is the
4 July card, handwritten; its change table reads NO CHANGE.

The 3 July card on frame 212 names seven of the eight men in the group action.
Smolek is the eighth and is on this sheet only. He appears on no other
transcribed frame. Frame 214 lists all eight by surname when they are relieved
attached and depart on 6 July.

The group line, verbatim: `Above 8 EM trfd to Inf Div Reorganizat-ional Det ?
APO 78 US Army per par 4 SO 73 Hq 32nd FA Brig APO 758 dtd 2 July 45 Atchd
pending departure (EDCMR 3 July 45)`. Rows carry the distilled action.

The single letter after `Det` is over-inked and reads P or F. APO 78 is the 78th
Infantry Division, which frames 197 and 198 also carry.

The 4 July card is signed Charles R Wood Jr, 1st Lt FA.

The printed strength grid on the 4 July card is not transcribed — the figures sit
in the lower bands and are left rather than guessed.
