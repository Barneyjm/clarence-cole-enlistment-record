First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each, both single sheets footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and both signed
GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA. Both are written by hand in block capitals. Frames
257 to 259 are typed.

The 6 September card is NO CHANGE.

Thorp leaves on 7 September. He joined on 1 September, frame 257, having been
assigned on 28 August, frame 254. His serial O1182727 and MOS 1193 match both
those frames. The order is SO 72 Hq 153rd FA Bn dtd 6 Sept 45; frame 259 carries
SO 71 of the day before, which took out Mulcahy, Cole, Chavez and Wilczynski.

The unit he goes to is written `246th Eng Combat Bn`. The middle digit is the
open-topped 4 this hand uses elsewhere on the card, in `34` on the essentials
line.

The station line spaces the grid reference as `wG 7875` on both cards. It is
recorded here closed up, as the typed cards carry it. The 6 September card writes
`Map` without a colon and the 7 September card `Map:`. Both are kept as written.

The code column carries a handwritten mark against Thorp's row that reads as a J
and a stroke.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tallies are in the
records of events. Both balance, and the officer figures fall by one across the
two days: 2 candidates and 1 essential against 3 officers assigned on 6
September, 1 and 1 against 2 on 7 September. Enlisted strength is 35 on both,
matching frame 259.
