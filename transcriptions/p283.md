First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each, both single sheets footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and both signed
GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA. Both change tables are written NO CHANGE, so
neither card carries rows. The frame is a dark, over-inked exposure and the
handwriting fills in.

The day field of the left card is written over at least two struck-out figures
and cannot be read. The day field of the right card carries one clean figure, a
7, formed like the 7 in `APO 752` on the station line of the left card and like
the 7 in `1700hrs` on frame 284. Frame 282 carries 5 and 6 October and frame 284
carries 9 and 10 October, so these two cards are 7 and 8 October; frames 280,
281, 282 and 284 each carry the earlier card on the left. The cards are dated on
that reading, which puts the right-hand card at 8 October against the 7 written
in its day field. A second reader should settle which of the two is wrong.

The left card's station is `Camp Pittsburgh Assembly Area APO 752`, as on frames
278 through 282. The right card's station is `Enroute To Staging Area`, with no
place name and no APO. Neither card carries a map citation or a grid reference.

The record of events on the right card gives the place of entrainment and the
hour. Both are over-inked and cannot be read; each stands as one question mark
in the quote below, which claims no length.

The redeployment tally is 4 officers and 109 enlisted men on both cards,
unchanged since 28 September on frame 278.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events on both cards.
