First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Sgt Cole is the only row on the 25 August card: `Fr TD to dy`, serial 36106875,
grade Sgt, all four fields clean. This is the film copy of the 25 August card,
previously held only as a separately supplied image. He returns from the leave
in Holland that began on the 20 August card, frame 243.

The 24 August card returns Minnick and Tierce. Minnick went to TD with the 3rd
QM Bn on 23 July (frame 225); Tierce on 13 August (frame 239). Their group line,
verbatim: `Above 2 EM fr TD to dy`. Rows carry the distilled action.

Minnick's first name is broken by a fold; `Vi` and `l C` survive. His serial
33399681 matches frames 225 and 250, which type the name Virgil C.

Tierce's serial 34332726 matches frames 233, 237, 239 and 250. Frame 208 read
34482726 for the same man on his assignment card and frame 243 could read only
3??32726. Frame 243 also types the surname `Pierce`; the first character here is
a plain T with the full crossbar, as on frames 233, 237, 239 and 250.

The 24 August card types `Map:` and the 25 August card `MAP:`. Both are kept as
typed.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tallies are in the
records of events, and both balance: 23 candidates and 44 essentials against 67
enlisted men assigned.
