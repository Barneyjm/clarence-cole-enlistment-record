First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two handwritten cards, one sheet each, both footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and signed GORDON
F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA. Both give the station as Camp Pittsburg Assembly Area APO
752, the first named station since the battery left Geismar on 18 September
(frame 273).

The 20 September change table carries one entry and no names or serial numbers:
`ASR Changes of O's & EM copy Atchd`. The attached copy is not on this frame.

The day field on the 21 September card is a heavy blot. It is read as 21 from the
sequence: 20 September is on the left of this frame and 18 and 19 September on
frame 273.

On the 20 September card the Candidates and Essentials lines of the redeployment
tally are written over and are not read. The total, 4 officers and 107 enlisted
men, is legible and is unchanged from 17 September.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower bands
and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record of
events on both cards.
