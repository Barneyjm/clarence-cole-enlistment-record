First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two handwritten cards, one sheet each, both signed GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA.
Both give the station as Geismar wG7875, as frames 263 and 264 do.

1st Lt Cook's serial is written here as an `O` followed by a shape like a capital
N and then 75405. Frame 258, his assignment card, gives O1175405. The N-shaped
character is left as `?`, so this reading is one character shorter than frame
258's. His MOS is 9312 here and 5000 on frame 258.

Billups and Poland are new to the record. The group line, verbatim: `Above 2 EM
Asgd & Jd fr Btry B 153RD FA Bn APO 758 per VOCO Hq 153RD FA Bn`. Rows carry the
distilled action.

The fifth digit of Poland's serial is a closed loop that reads as 0 or 9 and is
left as `?`. He is on no other transcribed frame.

Arnold's 19130187 matches frames 254 and 264, and the MOS 745 matches frame 264;
frame 254 gives 845. His 16 September entry closes the transfer that frame 264
carries as attached pending departure.

The code column holds a struck-through `K` against Cook and a two-character mark
against Billups.

The two tallies are consistent with the 14 September card on frame 264, which
records 3 officers and 95 enlisted men assigned: Cook's transfer takes the
officers to 2 and the two men from Battery B take the enlisted strength to 97.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower bands
and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record of
events on both cards.
