First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each. Both are single sheets footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and both are
signed GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA.

The 29 August card is under-inked and its one entry is the faintest reading in
this run. The sixth character of Brimlow's serial did not print cleanly and is
left as a question mark; frame 258, which transfers him to Battery A on
3 September, types O1185264. His MOS reads 0200 here and frame 258 types the
same.

The duty field on the fourth line of Brimlow's entry is typed over an earlier
entry and cannot be read. His ASR is typed `68-No`, the same truncated officer
form that frame 258 records for 1st Lt Cook.

The zone prefix of the grid reference printed only as a trace on the 29 August
card. The 30 August card types `Geismar wG7875 Nord de Guerre Zone`, as do
frames 247, 253, 254, 256, 257 and 258.

Brown's 32631774 matches frames 235 and 257. Frame 235, his assignment card of
7 August, gives ASR 55, which is the figure this card raises to 60.

On the 30 August card a fourth line was typed into the redeployment tally
between Candidates and Essentials and struck out.

A handwritten mark stands in the code column against Brown's row.

Frame 254, the day before, gives 3 officers assigned. Both cards here give 4.
Brimlow's assignment is the change.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tallies are in the
records of events, and both balance: 3 candidates and 1 essential against 4
officers assigned, 2 candidates and 37 essentials against 39 enlisted men
assigned.
