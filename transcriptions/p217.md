First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

The left sheet is a typed CONTINUATION of the 9 July report begun on frame 216,
headed CONTINUATION and footing PAGE 2 OF 2 PAGES. Its change table is empty; it
carries only the record of events and the map line, so the 9 July section below
holds no rows. The right sheet is the 10 July card, handwritten and signed
Gordon F. Kerkhoff, CAPT FA.

The 10 July card is a very faint exposure and much of the single entry cannot be
read. The man is McConnell, who joins on the strength of the 8 July report; frame
216 has him assigned not yet joined on 7 July with serial 34800821, and corrected
from Pvt to Pfc on 8 July. Here only `34?0082?` is legible. The middle line of the
entry begins Race W and is illegible beyond that; it sits on a printed rule. One
word between `Re` and `M/R` on the last line is also illegible.

The handwritten card ends its map line Sheet R-9. The typed continuation sheet on
the same frame reads Sheet R-3, as do the cards on frames 214, 216 and 218. Both
are left as written.

The last two digits of the handwritten grid reference are faint. They are read as
G8188, which the typed sheet on the same frame carries.

The typed sheet's place name is over-inked at the fourth letter and is read here
as Hershausen, as on frame 226; the handwritten card writes Herzhausen.

The printed strength grid on the 10 July card is not transcribed — the figures sit
in the lower bands and are left rather than guessed.
