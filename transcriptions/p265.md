RESTRICTED. HEADQUARTERS 29TH INFANTRY DIVISION, A.P.O. No. 29, U.S. ARMY.
"29 LET'S GO". SPECIAL ORDERS NUMBER 226. 11 Sept 1945.

> 1. Following named off and EM are reld fr asgmt to units indicated and are
> asgd to 153rd FA Bn APO 749. EDCMR: 13 Sept 1945. S/R and allied papers will
> be made current, carefully checked and will accompany pers. Auth: TWX Hq
> Seventh US Army No A25744 dated 8 Sept 1945.

The mirror of SO 66. Where that order sent the battalion's high-point men home,
this one fills the gaps with low-point men from the 29th Infantry Division,
which was itself going home. Note the ASR column: almost everyone here is in
the 60s and 70s, against 78 and up on SO 66.

Someone has written **BTRY C** by hand at the head of this copy.

Only the top of this page has been read. The rest of page 1 is still to do.
