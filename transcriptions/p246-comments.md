## The microfilm original of the 25 August card

Also held as a family photograph before the frame was read. Sgt Cole is the only
row: `Fr TD to dy`, back from the Treebeek leave.

The card names the map sheet in use — Marburg 1:100,000, Sheet R-3 — and counts
three officers and sixty-seven men on the rolls, from the hundred and eighteen
who sailed in May 1944.
