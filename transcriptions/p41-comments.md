# p41 — comments

## Ambrières is cited as 2G/3, and that is what the clerk wrote

Checked against the film at high magnification. The right card reads
`( Map Ambrieres Sheet 2G/3 )`, in the same curled hand as the 2G/1 he wrote for
Domfront on frame 40 two days earlier.

The GSGS 4250 index puts Ambrières on **7G/3**. The same substitution, twice in
one week.

A copy of the correct sheet is at the Vrijheidsmuseum via Collectie Gelderland,
linked from the sheet entry.
