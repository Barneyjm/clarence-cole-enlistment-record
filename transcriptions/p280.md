First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each, both single sheets footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and both signed
GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA. The 1 October card carries one entry; the
2 October change table is written NO CHANGE.

A narrow strip of a third card shows at the right edge of the frame. Nothing on
it is legible.

The station is spelled `Camp Pittsburgh` on both cards, as on frames 278 and 279.
Neither card carries a map citation or a grid reference.

Lambert returns to duty. His 34173673 matches frames 208, 243, 254 and 275, and
this row closes the admission on frame 275, where the 22 September card reads
`Fr dy To SK in Hosp (189th Gen Hosp)`. The 8 in the hospital number is written
narrow here and reads as a vertical stroke; frame 275 writes it the same way and
is the clearer exposure. He is `James L` here, as on frames 243, 254 and 275, and
`James I.` on frame 208.

The record of events on the 2 October card reads `Usual Assembly Area` and stops
there. Every other card in this range writes `Duties` after it.

The redeployment tally is 4 officers and 109 enlisted men on both cards,
unchanged since 28 September on frame 278.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events on both cards.
