First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each. Both are single sheets footed PAGE 1 OF 1, both are
signed GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA, and both change tables are written
NO CHANGE. Neither card names a man, so neither carries rows.

The station is Camp Pittsburg Assembly Area APO 752 on both cards, as on frame
275 and on the 24 September card of frame 276. Neither card carries a map
citation or a grid reference.

The redeployment tally is the same on both cards and the same as on 24 September:
4 officers and 107 enlisted men assigned, all 107 in the Candidates line.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed.
