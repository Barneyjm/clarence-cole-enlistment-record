# p40 — comments

## Domfront is cited as 2G/1, and that is what the clerk wrote

Checked against the film at high magnification. The right card reads
`(Map Domfront Sheet 2G/1)`.

The GSGS 4250 index puts Domfront on **7G/1**. 2G is in Brittany, around Brest,
several hundred miles from where the battery was.

This is the clerk's error rather than a misreading of it. The same hand writes
`1745 hrs` two lines above on the left card, and forms the 7 as a straight
diagonal with a top bar — nothing like the curled character in the citation.

The left card cites `(Map Mortain Sheet 6G/2)`, which is correct for Mortain.
