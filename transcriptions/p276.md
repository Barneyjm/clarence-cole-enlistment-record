First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each, both single sheets footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and both signed
GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA. The 23 September card is a copy: the signature is
written `s/Gordon F Kerkhoff`, and below the record of events the card carries
`A TRUE COPY` over a cursive `Gordon ? Howe WO Jg Adjutant`. The middle initial
is not legible.

The station is spelled `Camp Pittsborgh` on the 23 September card and
`Camp Pittsburg` on the 24 September card. Frame 275 also has `Camp Pittsburg`.
Neither card carries a map citation or a grid reference.

The 23 September card records five ASR changes and nothing else. Four are
69 to 70; Telles is 62 to 72.

Krayniak's 32918019 and Taylor's 34783149 match their rows on frame 269, and
Lesniak's 32919382 matches frame 270. Krayniak's MOS is 641 here against 650 on
frame 269; both readings are kept where they fall. Taylor's 641 agrees with
frame 269.

Telles is written `Telles, Ernesto V` here, serial 38213663, MOS 345. Frame 270
carries `Tellos, Ernesto V`, serial 36213663, MOS 677, and flags the serial. The
contested serial digit is the second. Both readings are kept where they fall.

Nebroski's serial is the faintest entry on the card and the last four digits
cannot be read. Frame 268 carries 32910859 for Nebroski, Frank, also flagged.

The four men whose ASR runs 69 to 70 here are carried at 62 on SO 226, frames
269 and 270, dated 11 September.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events on both cards.
