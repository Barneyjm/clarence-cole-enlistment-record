First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each, both single sheets footed PAGE 1 OF 1 and both signed
GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA. The 27 September card is written NO CHANGE and
carries no names. The 28 September card assigns two men.

The station is spelled `Camp Pittsborg` on the 27 September card and
`Camp Pittsburgh` on the 28 September card. Frames 275, 276 and 277 carry
`Camp Pittsburg` and, on 23 September, `Camp Pittsborgh`. Neither card here
carries a map citation or a grid reference.

The group line, verbatim: `Above 2 EM Asgd & Jd fr 67th Med Gp per par 1 SO 86
Hq Camp Pittsburgh ?ourmelon Sub Area APO 752 dtd 27 Sept 45 (EDCMR 27 Sept 45`.
Rows carry the distilled action. The closing parenthesis is not written. The
first letter of `?ourmelon` sits against the edge of the card and cannot be read.

Holder and Davis are both new to the record; neither serial and neither surname
appears on any other transcribed frame. Holder's serial and Davis's each read as
eight digits, and the third and fourth digits of Davis's are the weakest part of
that row.

The two digits of Holder's ASR cannot be read. Davis's ASR 87 on the row below is
clear.

Holder's row carries `A1` in the CODE column. Davis's CODE cell is empty.

The enlisted strength in the record of events goes from 107 on 27 September to
109 on 28 September, which is the two men joining.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events on both cards.
