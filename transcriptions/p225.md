First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Both cards on this frame are 23 July: the main card and its typed CONTINUATION
sheet, so the two sections below carry the same date and merge into one day.
Strength grids not transcribed.

The main card lists ten men typed in full — name, serial, grade, MOS — then the
group line `Above 10 EM trfd to Hq Btry 153rd FA Bn APO 758 U S Army per VOCO
153rd FA Bn Departed`. Each row below carries the distilled action, p6-style,
with the MOS appended; the verbatim group line lives here.

Minnick's row is the last on the main card and his action line is the group
entry that opens the continuation card: `Above 2 EM fr dy to TD with 3rd QM Bn
per VOCO 32nd FA Brig APO 758 US Army Approx duration 7 days Departed` — the
two EM being Minnick and Warren.

Warren is typed `Warren Charles D` here; frame 147 read the same serial
34349110 as `Warren, Chandler D.` The two sources disagree on the given name
and both readings are kept where they fall.
