First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

One card on two sheets, not two cards. The left sheet foots PAGE 1 OF 2 and is
signed GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA; the right is headed CONTINUATION and foots
PAGE 2 OF 2, carries no signature, and holds the record of events. Both give the
station as Geismar wG7875.

The day field is written 17 on the continuation sheet and 19 on page 1. The card
is filed as 17 September: the change table runs across both sheets, and frame 273
carries separate cards for 18 and 19 September.

Fourteen men in three groups. The group lines, verbatim:

- `Above 2 O's Asgd & Jd fr 1st Armored Div APO 251 US Army per par 4 SO 235 Hq
  1st Armored Div dtd 14 Sept 45 (EDCMR 17 Sept 45)`
- `Above 9 EM Asgd & Jd fr 29th Inf Div APO 29 US Army per par 1 SO 226 Hq 29
  Inf Div dtd 11 Sept 45 (EDCMR 13 Sept 45)`
- `Above 2 EM Asgd & Jd fr 1st Armored Div APO 251 US Army per par 2 SO 235 Hq
  1st Armored Div dtd 14 Sept 45 (EDCMR 17 Sept 45)`

Rows carry the distilled action. The nine men of the second group are split five
on page 1 and four on page 2.

Eight of those nine are on the transcribed pages of Special Orders 226, which is
the order the card cites. Sabala's 39828769, MOS 667 and ASR 81 match frame 266
in every field. Kreig's 16085434, MOS 776 and ASR 81 match frame 267, and
Curley's 35627954 and MOS 590 match frame 268. Nedroski is on none of the
transcribed order pages.

Four fields disagree with the order and are recorded as this card has them:

- Brock's serial 34530151 matches frame 267, which gives his ASR as 64 and his
  grade as Tec 5. This card gives S Sgt, and a pen stroke crosses the ASR figure.
- Kreig is Hilton J here and Milton J on frame 267.
- Cole's serial reads 36857286 here and 36857203 on frame 267. The MOS 803 and
  ASR 72 agree.
- Curley's ASR reads with a struck first digit here; frame 268 gives 74.

Hickman's serial runs two characters together in the middle. Frame 268 gives
39197929, itself flagged, which fits what is legible.

Kleidon's serial is written with nine digits, 366113166. Frame 269 carries him as
Kleiden with the partial 3?61?1??, which fits an eight-digit reading of the same
figure.

Agee's serial is smudged in the middle and his ASR is covered by an ink blot.
Frame 266 gives 34520807 for Agee, Jr, Ivy C with ASR 82.

The surname of the first officer is read Hospor; its first, fourth and fifth
letters are indistinct and he is on no other transcribed frame. Lague's surname
and the first digits of his serial are crossed by a pen stroke from the line
above.

Billups was assigned from Battery B on 15 September (frame 271) and leaves two
days later. His 6249131 is the same on both cards.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower bands
and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record of
events, and it follows from the 15 and 16 September cards: two officers become
four, and 97 enlisted men become 107 with nine and two joining and Billups
leaving.
