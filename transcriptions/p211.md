First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

One report on two sheets, not two cards. The right-hand sheet is headed
CONTINUATION and foots PAGE 2 OF 2 against PAGE 1 OF 2 on the left, and repeats
the 1 July header and the Geismar station line. The change table runs across
both. Both sheets are typed. The left sheet is signed Charles R Wood Jr, 1st Lt
FA.

Four men go to four different battalions on the same day, each under its own
paragraph of the same order: Martin to the 979th under par 5, Chamberlain to the
978th under par 6, Drofits to the 203rd under par 7, and Carlson and Trask to the
696th Armd under par 8, all of SO 70, Hq 32nd FA Brig, dtd 29 June 45. Bassett
goes to the 551st under a different order, par 3 SO 71 dtd 30 June 45.

The Carlson and Trask group line, verbatim: `Above 2 EM trfd to 696th Armd FA Bn
APO 758 US Army per par 8 SO 70 Hq 32nd FA Brig APO 758 dtd 29 June 45 Departed
(EDCMR 1 July 45)`. Rows carry the distilled action. Carlson is the last row on
sheet 1 and the group line opens sheet 2.

Martin's entry types `Dy to trfd tc 979th`. The other three type `to`. Kept as
typed.

Bassett's serial is typed O1173865. Frame 208 reads O1173565 for the same man,
who joined the battery four days earlier. The fifth digit is the contested one
and both readings are kept where they fall.

Drofits is typed Drofits Mike here. Frame 23 carries the same serial 33484574
under the surname Droelitz.

Chamberlain is typed Chamberlain Edward HJr here, with the initial and the
suffix run together. Frame 113 carries the same serial 35790738 as Chamberlain
Jr, S. H.

Carlson's 37087219 matches frame 113 and Trask's 15354642 matches frame 121.
Martin is new to the record.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands of sheet 1 and are left rather than guessed.
