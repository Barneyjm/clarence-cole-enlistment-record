First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Frame 158 carries two cards, Btry C 153rd FA Bn: 27 March 1945 on the left and
28 March 1945 on the right. Those are the two cards already recorded in p157.md,
so no rows are repeated here.

The files and the frames are out of step across this stretch of film. Read off
the images:

- frame 155 — 23 and 24 March
- frame 156 — 23 and 24 March, a second exposure
- frame 157 — 25 and 26 March
- frame 158 — 27 and 28 March
- frame 159 — 29 and 30 March

p155.md and p159.md sit on their own frames. p156.md holds the 25 and 26 March
cards, which are on frame 157. p157.md holds the 27 and 28 March cards, which
are on this frame. Frame 156 has no file. Correcting this means renumbering
p156.md and p157.md, which is outside a transcription pass.

Three things on this frame that p157.md does not carry. The 27 March change
table reads `NO CHANGE`, and below it both cards carry
`Limited Asgmt Pers / Officers-0 WO-0 EM-0`. The 27 March station line runs
longer than the grid reference alone — a place name and an offset precede
`wF7274` — and those opening words are too faint on this exposure to read.
p157.md gives the station as `wF7274 Nord de Guerre Zone (Germany)`.

The 28 March card names 1st Lt Wood, Charles R. Jr. This exposure reads his
serial O1174476, which matches frames 99, 161 and 218. Its station line is
`Hanf wF7234 Nord de Guerre Zone`.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed.
