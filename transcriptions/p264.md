First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two sheets. The left one is headed CONTINUATION and footed PAGE 2 OF 2; it is
page 2 of the typed 13 September card that begins on frame 263, and it carries
the record of events and the redeployment tally for that day. It has no
signature block. The right card is 14 September, handwritten, a single sheet
signed GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA.

The day field of the continuation sheet is typed 13 over a struck-out 12.

The continuation opens with the tail of the group line broken off the foot of
frame 263: `Bn APO 758 dtd 13 Sept 45 Departed (EDCMR 13 Sept 45)`.

Weitz's 32826608 matches frames 163, 165, 170, 177, 202 and 254. Frame 165
records his duty changed fr 590 to 8531; this card gives 590.

Arnold's 19130187 matches frame 254, his assignment card, which gives his MOS as
845. This card gives 745. Both readings are kept where they fall.

Weitz goes to the 1st Armd Div Arty under paragraph 1 of SO 75 and Arnold to the
2nd Armd Div Arty under paragraph 2 of the same order — the paragraph that moved
the five men on frame 263. Arnold is carried attached pending departure; the
others departed. The code column carries a handwritten `K` and a stroke against
both rows.

Capt Berry's serial O1171897, MOS 1981-6 and ASR 121 match Special Orders 226 on
frame 265, which is the order his entry cites. His entry gives the EDCMR as 13
September, the date SO 226 sets.

The 14 September card records 60 enlisted men assigned and joined under the same
paragraph of SO 226 and notes a copy attached. It names none of them, so the row
below carries no name and no serial. A pen stroke crosses the code column beside
it.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events on both sheets: 2 officers and 26 enlisted men assigned on 13
September, 3 officers and 95 enlisted men on 14 September. The card records 60
enlisted men joining against a rise of 69.
