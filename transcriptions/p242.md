frame 212 carried, against the O1996670 that frame 221 flagged. His transfer to

on this card the Candidates figure is struck over from 4 to 3.

DS both fit the space, so it stays a double question mark. The school site is
typed with a faint head but the tail `...erammergau, Germany` admits only
Oberammergau.
