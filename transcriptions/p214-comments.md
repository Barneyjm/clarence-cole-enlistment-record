## Herzhausen: the grid and the gazetteer disagree, and neither is settled

The battery gives Herzhausen as its station from 6 July 1945, and holds the
position through 25 August — 46 days, and the ground Sgt Cole was on for both
cards that name him.

Two readings appear:

- 6 July, `Herzhausen G8188` — no 500 km square letter, so it cannot be decoded
  on its own.
- 17 July onward, `Herzhausen wG8188` — decodes to **51.1889 N, 8.9009 E**.

That point is 14.6 km from the gazetteer's `Herzhausen, Hesse` at 51.0803,
8.7842, so the build lists it and refuses to plot it.

**Hesse has more than one Herzhausen, and checking made it worse rather than
better.** Vöhl-Herzhausen, at the western end of the Edersee, sits near 9.06 E.
That matches neither the decoded grid nor the gazetteer. So there are at least
three candidate points and no basis yet for choosing between them.

Do not adjust the gazetteer to make this agree. What would settle it: read the
Marburg 1:100,000 sheet R.3 directly, which is on the site at 3000 px, and find
which Herzhausen falls in grid square 81/88. The battery's other position on the
same sheet, Geismar `wG7875`, decodes to 51.0724, 8.8553 and agrees with its
village, so the square letters and the decoding are working.
