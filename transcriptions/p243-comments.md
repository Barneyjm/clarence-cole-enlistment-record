## The microfilm original of a card previously held only as a photograph

This is the 20 August 1945 card, supplied separately by the family as an image
before the frame was read. Both now exist, so the entry is double-sourced.

Sgt Cole is the first row: five days' temporary duty at Treebeek, Holland, on a
leave order from the 32nd Field Artillery Brigade dated 16 August, departed
19 August.

The card types `Approx suration 5 days`, for *duration*. Left as written.
