Second exposure of the two cards on frame 226 — 24 July (Goad) and 25 July
(Plunkett, Turnbaugh, Baroglio) — and much the cleaner of the pair. Rows are
recorded on p226, corrected against this frame: it settles Turnbaugh as
33386924 and the name spelling Baroglio. Its reading of Baroglio's last serial
digit as 9, against frame 226's apparent 0, is confirmed by the typed card on
frame 232 — 39577769.
