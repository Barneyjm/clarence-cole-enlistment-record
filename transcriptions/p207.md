First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two sheets carrying two different reports. The left sheet is headed CONTINUATION
and foots PAGE 2 OF 2; it holds the rest of the 25 June change table, whose page
1 is on frame 206. The right sheet is the 26 June card, typed, PAGE 1 OF 1,
signed Charles R Wood Jr, 1st Lt FA.

The left sheet is handwritten in a light hand and the film is near its
resolution limit there. The right sheet is typed and clear.

The 25 June group line, verbatim: `Above 6 EM Asgd & Jd Fr 575th FA Bn APO 339
US ARMY Per Par 7 SO 64 Hq 32d FA Brigade APO 758 US ARMY dtd 23 June 45 (EDCMR
25 June 45)`. Rows carry the distilled action. Four of the six men are on this
sheet; the other two are on page 1, frame 206. Each man's second line gives race,
MCO, ASR and physical class, and those are folded into his row.

The 575th FA Bn appears on both sides of the same day's report: page 1 sends
eight men to it, page 2 receives six men from it. The APO differs between the
two sheets, 758 on page 1 and 339 here.

Larsen's serial reads 36239144, the figure frame 225 carries for the man it
types Larson, Clarence A. The vowel in the surname on this sheet is not legible
enough to settle the spelling, so both readings stand where they fall.

Sobel is typed Kenneth F here and Kenneth R on frame 225. The seventh digit of
his serial is a 4 or an 8 and is left as a question mark; frame 225 reads
32953484.

Yurasek is typed William D here and William P on frame 225. The third digit of
his serial is a 7 or a 9 and is left as a question mark; frame 225 reads
33762845, which differs from this sheet in three places.

Sobel's physical class letter reads N. The other three read A.

Lovelace is new to the record.

The 26 June card types Ford's serial O1170465. Frame 208 carries O1173465 for
him, and frames 168 and 184 carry 0469891. The fifth character here is plainly a
zero.

Daugherty's fifth character is blotted and is left as a question mark. Frames
208, 212 and 242 read O1196670.

Hamlin's O1176045 matches frames 9 and 208. His MOS sits against the ruled edge
of the card and its second digit cannot be read.

Ford and Hamlin are both carried `Atchd pending departure` on 26 June. Frame 208
records them relieved and departed on the 28 June card.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures are left rather
than guessed.
