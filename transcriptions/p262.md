First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, one day each. Both are handwritten, both are single sheets footed
PAGE 1 OF 1, and both are signed GORDON F KERKHOFF, Capt, FA. The 11 September
card is written NO CHANGE and carries no entries.

The 10 September group line, verbatim: `Above 2 EM Trfd To 32nd FA Brig APO 758
US Army Per par 1 SO 110, Hq 32nd FA Brig dtd 10 Sept 45. Departed (EDCMR 10
Sept 45.` The opening bracket before EDCMR is never closed. Rows carry the
distilled action.

Baroglio's 39577769 matches frames 175, 226, 227, 232 and 244.

Sheehan is `Sheehan, William L` on frame 254, his assignment card, which reads
his serial 31168359 and his MOS 745. The last digit of the serial here is
over-inked and is left as a question mark. His MOS on this card reads 915;
both readings are kept where they fall.

The MOS column is written 745 against Baroglio. Frame 175, his assignment card,
gives `MOO 482`.

On the 11 September card a character between `Sheet` and `R-3` in the map
citation is struck out by overwriting and cannot be read.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events on both cards and gives 2 officers and 33 enlisted men assigned on
each. Frame 261 gives 35 enlisted men assigned on 9 September; the two transfers
are the difference.
