Special Orders 226, Hq 29th Infantry Division, 11 September 1945. Frames 265–270.

## First pass, not verified

None of these pages has been through `verify-transcription`. The order is long,
and several frames are damaged along the left edge.

**24 of the 241 men have a serial number that is not fully legible**, carried as
`?` characters — `3?9?4?74`, `330?????`. A partial serial cannot act as an
identity key, so the build keeps those rows out of the cross-check, which means
they get none of its protection. They are the most valuable rows to fix.

## What the order is

The mirror of SO 66. Where August sent the battalion's high-point men home, this
assigns 241 low-point men from the 29th Infantry Division, which was itself going
home. Almost everyone leaving on SO 66 had 78 points or more; almost everyone
arriving here had fewer.

Authority is Seventh US Army; the order is signed by command of Major General
Gerhardt.

## Watch for the second Cole

established and none should be implied. Match on serial, never on surname.
