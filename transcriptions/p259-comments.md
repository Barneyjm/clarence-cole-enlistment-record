# p259 — comments

## Cole leaves Battery C, a month before he sails

The most consequential card for this site outside the family papers, and it
corrected a claim the site had been making.

> Cole, Clarence J 36106875 Sgt — Trfd to 3rd Reinf Depot APO 153 US Army per
> par 1 SO 71 Hq 153rd FA Bn APO 758 dtd 5 Sept 45 Departed (EDCMR 5 Sept 45).
> MOS 845

He is transferred out with Chavez and Wilczynski, effective 5 September 1945. His
discharge has him sailing on 7 October, so the last month of his overseas service
was not spent with the battery this record follows. Until this frame was read the
site said he stayed with Battery C to the end.

## His military occupation is recorded twice, differently

This card gives **845**. His discharge gives **Squad Leader, 653**. Both are shown
on the site and neither is preferred; there is no basis here for choosing.

Worth knowing when reading the roster: 845 is far and away the most common MOS on
Special Orders 66, which suggests it is a common transport or driving trade rather
than anything specific to him.
