First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

The film photographed this page twice. Frame 12 carries the same two cards as
frame 13 — 14 June 1944 handwritten on the left, 15 June 1944 typed on the
right, Btry C at Branksome-Hants — and is much the darker of the two exposures.
Whole typed lines on the right-hand card fill in solid. Rows are not repeated
here; they live on p13.

Two readings this frame bears on.

Causey's entry on the 14 June card ends `dtd 13 June 44`. The digit is legible
on this exposure. p13.md records `dtd 14 June 44` for the same row. The 15 June
card settles which is which: it quotes the 14 June entry as reading
`dtd 13 June 44` and then corrects it to `dtd 14 June 44`.

That correction sequence is on both exposures of the 15 June card and is not in
p13.md. The card runs: Leonard's transfer to US Army Hosp Plant 4119, then
`So much of M/R on 14 June 44 reads` above the Causey entry as originally filed,
then `Corrected to read` above the amended entry. Read it from frame 13, which
is legible.

The Record of Events block of the 15 June card is blank on both exposures.
p13.md records `No change.` for that card.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed.
