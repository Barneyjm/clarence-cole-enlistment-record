First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

One card on two sheets, not two cards. The left sheet foots PAGE 1 OF 2 and the
right is headed CONTINUATION and foots PAGE 2 OF 2. The continuation carries the
redeployment tally and nothing else.

The station is Geismar. The battery moved there from Herzhausen on 26 August,
frame 247.

The left sheet types the grid reference without a legible prefix — the
characters before 7875 did not print. The continuation sheet types
`Geismar wG7875 Nord de Guerre Zone`, which is the station recorded here.
Frames 195 through 201 type the same square as `G7875` and add `(Germany)`.

Seven men leave the battery for Service Battery on this card. The group line,
verbatim: `Above 6 EM trfd to Serv Btry 153rd FA Bn APO 758 US Army per VOCO
153rd FA Bn Departed`. Rows carry the distilled action. Chmielewski is entered
separately below it because he was on temporary duty rather than with the
battery.

Ferguson's serial 37187760 matches frame 236. His first name is over-inked at
the initial and reads Elmer here; frame 236 could read only `?mer C`.

Watson is typed 33519323. Frame 46 carries 33819323 for the same man, entered
there as `Watson G`; the third digit is the contested one. This exposure reads
5. Both readings are kept where they fall.

Himmelreich is typed 33483609, which matches frame 23 and settles the fifth
digit that frame 231 could read only as `3348?609`.

Norris is typed with MOS 606. Frame 234, his assignment card, gives MOS 605.
His serial 33399076 matches that frame.

Chmielewski is typed 35761991, as on frame 243. Frames 2, 4 and 113 carry
35261991 for the same man. Two occupation-period frames now read 7 in the third
place.

Turnbaugh's 33386924 matches frames 147, 226, 227 and 244. Foltz's 16100806
matches frames 46, 53, 54 and 58.

A handwritten mark stands in the code column against Ferguson's row and against
Chmielewski's, and a pen stroke runs down the code column beside the four rows
between them.

The printed strength grid is not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tally is in the record
of events, and it balances: 2 candidates and 29 essentials against 31 enlisted
men assigned.
