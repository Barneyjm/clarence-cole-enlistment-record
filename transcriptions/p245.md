First pass, read from deskewed bands. Not yet checked by a second reader.

Two cards, both NO CHANGE. No man is named on either.

The 22 August card is a heavy over-inked exposure and the 23 August card beside
it is clean. The two carry the same station, the same map citation and the same
tally, and the tally on both balances: 23 candidates and 44 essentials against
67 enlisted men assigned.

The fourth letter of the station name on the 22 August card is a blot. It is
struck as one angular character, unlike the rounded `s` of `hausen` on the same
line, and the 23 August card types `Herzhausen` plainly.

The record of events reads `duties` on the 22 August card and `Duties` on the
23 August card. Both are kept as typed.

The printed strength grids are not transcribed — the figures sit in the lower
bands and are left rather than guessed. The redeployment tallies are in the
records of events.
