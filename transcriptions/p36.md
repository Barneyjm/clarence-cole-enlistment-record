First pass, transcribed from the film. Not yet checked by a second reader.

The right-hand card's day glyph is ambiguous between 8 and 9. It is read here as
8 August because p37 carries 9 August with different content (Brown admitted to
hospital at Chaisel, where this card is the move to Chaisel), and because no
other frame carries 8 August at all. The reading rests on that, not on the glyph.
