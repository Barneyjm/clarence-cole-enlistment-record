Covers the St Lô run, frames 26–30.

## The sheet is cited as 6E/2, and that is what the clerk wrote

Checked against the film at high magnification on this frame, where the pencil is
clearest: the citation reads `Map St Lo Sheet 6E/2` and the letter has the three
strokes of a capital E. Compare the E in `RECORD OF EVENTS` on the same card.

St Lô is sheet **6F/2** in the GSGS 4250 index, and the series has no 6E/2. The
clerk wrote the wrong designation. He uses 6F/2 from 28 July, on frame 31.

Both readings are kept. The transcription is faithful; the error is his.

Frame 26 carries the same citation in pencil, struck through and overwritten, and
is not legible on its own. Read frame 29 instead if this needs checking again.
