and are left rather than guessed.

The 20 July card is typed, not handwritten — the first typed card in this run —
and it settles a flagged reading: p220 could only manage `Gorson F` for the
captain's name, and this card types `Kerkhoff Gordon F`. It also types the
station `Hershausen`, where the handwritten cards have `Herzhausen`; both
spellings kept as written. The 19 July card is signed `Gordon F. Kerkhoff, Capt`
rather than the usual Lt Mulcahy; the 20 July card is Mulcahy again.

A handwritten `11-8` sits in the right margin beside the Folts row, outside the
code column.
