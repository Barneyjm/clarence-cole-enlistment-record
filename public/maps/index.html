The Maps

Every morning report ends its record of events by naming the map sheet in use — (Map Bonn 1:100,000 Sheet S-1.) — and opens its station line with a position on that sheet. Between them they give a firing position to the kilometre, on a named map, on a dated day.

The firing positions

Two grid systems appear in the reports: Lambert Zone I in Normandy, and the Nord de Guerre zone from the Seine onward. Both need a datum shift of more than a kilometre to match a modern map. The lettered squares are not taken from a manual — they are worked out from the reports, by requiring each village the battery named to agree with the square written beside it.
