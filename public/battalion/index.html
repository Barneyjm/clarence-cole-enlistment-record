The men

The Battalion, by the numbers

Two orders, three weeks apart, replaced most of the battalion. On 24 August 1945 Special Orders 66 transferred 142 men to the 70th Infantry Division for return to the United States. On 11 September the 29th Infantry Division, which was also going home, assigned 241 of its men to the 153rd. The two orders name 383 soldiers.

The point scores explain the exchange. Almost every man leaving on SO 66 had 78 or more. Almost every man arriving on SO 226 had fewer. Men with enough points went home. Men without them were transferred to units that stayed.

Each dot is one man, keyed to his Army serial number. These documents record just one relationship between any two of them — that they held the same military occupational specialty, and so did the same job — so that is what the lines mean. Men who worked alongside each other cluster together.
